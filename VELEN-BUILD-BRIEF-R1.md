## Inherited contract

Keep quests central. Horizontal story backbone, one open hub, fixed home for each task, shared completion, exact tree return, stage resume independent of completion. Categories inside a quest hub: walkthrough, side quests, contracts, POIs/exploration and gear when relevant. Recommended order minimizes backtracking. Main map retains the full region with filters. Unknown story facts stay unknown until the player records them.

## Start at the actual transition

Treat Imperial Audience in Vizima as the short bridge into Velen, rather than silently jumping from the White Orchard inn to Hanged Man's Tree. First Velen slice: The Nilfgaardian Connection, from Hanged Man's Tree through the Inn at the Crossroads and Hendrik's investigation in Heatherton. The next story leads include Bloody Baron and Hunting a Witch. The branch order remains free-form; recommended route is guidance, not a lock.

First build boundary: bridge + this first main-quest hub and its verified practical opportunities. Do not claim the full Velen region, all map pins or all side quests are complete. Content and quest-failure logic must be source-checked before import.
