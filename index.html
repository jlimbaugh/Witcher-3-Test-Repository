TWISTED FIRESTARTER / WALKTHROUGH

Follow
the embers.

An investigation through the village.
Clues first. Consequences when you choose.

Return to hubRegional map
Stage 1 of 4
01

Begin at the forge

Agree to investigate for Willis. Search behind his workshop with Witcher Senses.

View the starting location →
02

Pick up the trail

Follow the footprints toward the water. Continue around the bridge and examine the signs of injury; be ready for drowners.

03

Find the wounded villager

Use the remaining tracks to find the suspect in the village. Save before the confrontation.

Reveal the suspect and your options · Spoilers

Napp offers a bribe. Accept it to conceal his involvement, or refuse and bring him to Willis. Axii provides a way to secure his cooperation.

Reveal the consequences · Major quest spoiler

Taking the bribe lets Napp escape punishment. Returning him to Willis brings the Nilfgaardian soldiers into the matter and leads to his execution.

04

Return to Willis

Resolve the investigation with Willis according to your choice.

DISCOVERY / PLACE OF POWER

Among
the stones.

A permanent reward, guarded by an unsettled spirit.

Return to hubRegional map

Location

At the cemetery north of the Mill signpost in White Orchard.

Preparation

Expect a wraith encounter. Check your silver sword and specter tools before approaching.

Action

Before approaching: the referenced encounter is level 7. The route’s level allowance is not a guarantee of an easy fight; upscaling and edition differences require a live check.

Deal with the immediate threat, then draw from the Place of Power.

Reward

The first draw grants an ability point. The Sign buff is temporary.
