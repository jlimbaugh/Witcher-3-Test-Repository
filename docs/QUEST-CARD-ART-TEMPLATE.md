# Quest and activity card art template

Owner decision, November 18, 2026. Applies to new cards and art replacements across Phoenix.

| Card | Required subject |
| --- | --- |
| Main or side quest | A character, creature, object, or recognizable scene specific to the named quest |
| Witcher contract | The actual target enemy, with the verified variant where applicable |
| Bandit camp | Bandits from that camp’s encounter, with the site as context |
| Treasure chest or cache | The chest or cache at that stop, with recognizable surroundings |
| Monster nest | The particular nest at that stop, showing its identifiable setting and corresponding monster type when useful |

Before assigning art, record the card ID, verified subject and location, gameplay or reference source, asset path, provenance, and review status. Check the crop in the phone deck so the subject remains recognizable. Use the accepted regional art treatment and avoid revealing a late quest outcome on the card face. A single subject can serve the card and its walkthrough header when the crop works for both.

A generic regional landscape means art is pending. Card and walkthrough acceptance do not clear that pending art check.
