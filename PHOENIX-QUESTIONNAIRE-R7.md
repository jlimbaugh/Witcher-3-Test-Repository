# Phoenix — accepted questionnaire rules R7

Owner: CaptDrunk · Builder: Cmdr Chad · 12 November 2026

These decisions supersede conflicting R1–R6 interaction notes.

## Quest hubs and routes

- Main map contains all marker types. Services, merchants, signposts and equipment stations are map utilities, not quest checklist rows.
- Quest walkthrough remains visible. Other groups start collapsed, may be open together, and restore their open state and exact tree position after guide visits.
- Group headings show completed/total, with a check at full completion. Completed rows and groups stay in route order.
- Recommendations prioritize convenience and less backtracking. They are opportunities, not enforced player orders.
- An activity can appear in multiple suitable clusters. Every occurrence opens the same guide and edits one shared completion record. Primary home remains a stable fallback for direct links.

## Maps and services

- Hub → map shows only that cluster, with Show all markers available.
- Quest/discovery pins open their guide directly. Utility pins open a compact panel.
- Return from the guide restores map position, zoom and cluster/filter context.
- Merchant panels show name and known flags for cards, quest items, unique items or quest involvement. No inventory list. Economy → Merchants provides drill-down.

## Recorded progression

- Selecting/browsing a quest never advances saved story progress. The player records it explicitly.
- Contextual progress controls plus an always-available progress deck support updates.
- Unknown prerequisites hide extra opportunities and offer a compact confirmation prompt. Known blocked opportunities stay out of clusters. Main map retains their guide access.
- Consequential story milestones implied by a quest update require review in the same confirmation.
- Major transition warnings list only unfinished quests at risk of failure/unavailability, with direct links; no forced gate on continuing.

## Objective cards

- Horizontal left/right deck; one objective per card; chapter labels, search and jump for long quests.
- Deck starts collapsed behind Quest progress · completed/required. Opens on the first unfinished available objective.
- A checkbox means completed or not. Browsing and walkthrough Next never mark completion.
- Required count follows the chosen path. Optional count stays separate. Closed optional objectives remain visible as unavailable and leave the available denominator.
- Final required objective completes the quest as part of the same reviewed update. Overall quest completion offers a catch-up review.
- Earlier required prerequisites are preselected in catch-up. Optional and unchosen branches are excluded. Missing choices are asked in the same prompt, with outcome explanations concealed.
- One Undo reverses the entire most recent update, including milestones, completion and route notices.
- Unchecking previews dependent corrections before confirmation.
- Closed alternate cards stay subdued and labeled unavailable. Failed quests remain visible and distinctly labeled.

## Route updates

One compact strip above the tree combines new opportunities. Opening a notice navigates to the affected hub/group and highlights activities. That clears the notice without completing activities or automatically moving the player at notification time.

## Acceptance boundary

White Orchard remains the pilot. The R7 mechanics are implemented against a bounded authored quest model. Exact Xbox journal wording, all alternative prerequisite paths, encounter levels and live story conditions still require a content audit. The source-linked card labels are shortened; do not call them verbatim journal transcription.
